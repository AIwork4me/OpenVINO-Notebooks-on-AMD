## Shared-helper impact analysis

### `utils/notebook_utils.py` — `download_file()`

Behavior change is **repair-scoped**: downloads now stream to `<name>.part`,
enforce the server-declared content length at the urllib3 transport layer, and
atomically replace the destination; a previously interrupted/corrupt cache file
can no longer be silently reused. For any workload whose artifacts are already
complete on disk the observable behavior is unchanged (the exists-check short
circuit precedes the streaming block).

- 66 upstream notebooks call `download_file`.
- Materially affected = only workloads whose recorded failures match the
  corrupted-artifact signature. Evidence-derived cluster (`MODEL_ARTIFACT_INCOMPLETE_OR_CORRUPT`
  in `results/marathon-state.json`, cross-checked against per-run stderr —
  Gate 2 widened this from the initial 4):

